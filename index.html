Top 5 Man City Facts

  • Founded in 1880 as St. Mark’s (West Gorton)
  • Moved to the Etihad Stadium in 2003
  • Won the Premier League 9 times (and counting!)
  • Pep Guardiola became manager in 2016
  • First English team to win the domestic treble (2018–19)
